# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Household shoppers and farmers use AgroMart as buyers. Household shoppers buy fresh produce; farmers buy agricultural supplies.

## Product Purpose

AgroMart is an online store for fresh produce and agricultural essentials. It supports browsing a product catalog, managing a cart, checking out, and viewing an order confirmation.

## Positioning

The catalog brings household groceries and farm supplies together in one storefront.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse and search products, filter by category, add produce items, and place orders with delivery and payment options. The site includes local cart, account, and order storage in the browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current web app includes product discovery, category filters, cart management, account forms, multiple checkout options, delivery selection, produce-item entry, and order confirmation. Preserve the existing product catalog and working interactions during the UI redesign. Payment and account flows are implemented in the current app; no production payment, identity, sourcing, certification, or delivery claims are established by the available project evidence.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is AgroMart. Existing product names, catalog data, and the Indian rupee pricing format are part of the current storefront.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current storefront, product catalog, and product imagery. It does not contain independently verifiable proof for claims such as certification, organic sourcing, farmer margins, or delivery performance; do not strengthen or invent such claims.

## Product Principles

- Make fresh produce and agricultural supplies straightforward to find.
- Keep prices, quantities, and checkout totals legible.
- Preserve access to existing shopping and account tasks.
- Distinguish implemented capabilities from claims that lack evidence.
